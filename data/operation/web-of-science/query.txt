I prepared the four queries for Web of Science and saved them in this `query.txt` file; I included both the full expressive versions and the short variants I used while testing.

1) MPC — full query I used

TS=(
  ("electric vehicle" OR EV OR PEV OR BEV OR "plug-in")
  AND (charg* OR "smart charging" OR "managed charging" OR "charging scheduling" OR EVSE OR "charging station" OR "charging infrastructure")
  AND ("battery energy storage" OR BESS OR ESS OR "energy storage system" OR "stationary battery" OR "grid battery")
  AND ("real-time" OR realtime OR online OR "rolling horizon" OR "receding horizon" OR dispatch OR "energy management" OR control OR scheduling OR "operational")
  AND ("model predictive control" OR MPC OR "receding horizon control" OR "rolling horizon")
  AND (coordinat* OR "joint optimization" OR co-optim* OR "integrated" OR "multi-objective" OR "coupled")
)
AND NOT TS=(planning OR siting OR placement OR sizing OR "capacity planning" OR expansion OR investment OR layout OR "infrastructure planning")
AND PY=2015-2025
AND DT=(Article)
AND WC=(ENGINEERING ELECTRICAL ELECTRONIC OR ENERGY FUELS)

Short version I used when necessary:
TS=(("electric vehicle" OR EV) AND (BESS OR "battery energy storage") AND (charg* OR charging) AND ("real-time" OR dispatch) AND (MPC OR "model predictive"))
